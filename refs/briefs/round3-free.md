# AppTime — round 3, variante livre (janela nova; mesmo texto no Claude Design e no Stitch)

Design the whole Android app "AppTime" as you think it should be. Brazilian Portuguese UI text. Phone artboards, 360×800 dp. Label every artboard.

What the product is (fixed):
- It reduces phone addiction through awareness. It never blocks anything.
- There are no goals. The only reference is the person's own "habitual": their average daily use over the last 30 days, per app and for the whole phone.
- A floating overlay (already designed elsewhere; do not redesign it) shows today's time in the app in front. Short messages (≤15 words) appear when a session passes 20 min, an app passes its habitual, or the phone passes its habitual — each at most once a day.
- Everything stays on the device: no network, no account. The day turns at 04:00.
- It has a light and a dark theme, following the system setting; show the main screens in both.
- It needs Android permissions (overlay, usage access, autostart on Xiaomi, no hibernation), and the system sometimes kills it silently.

What is open (everything else): which screens exist, how they are navigated, what each shows, the visual language, which data is worth showing. The attached screenshots are today's app — what it measures and how it looks now; you may keep, cut or replace any of it.

Deliver: the main screens, the first-run setup, the state of the first days when there is no habitual yet, and the state when tracking stopped. Then, in a few lines, the three decisions you made that someone following the current app would not have made, and why.
