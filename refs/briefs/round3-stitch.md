# AppTime — round 3: prompts para Google Stitch (colar um por vez, modo App/mobile)

## Prompt 1 — contexto e estilo (colar primeiro, com as capturas anexas)
Android app "AppTime", Brazilian Portuguese UI, light and dark themes following the system (sent as a follow-up). It reduces phone addiction through awareness, never blocking. The only reference is the user's own "habitual" = average daily use over the last 30 days; there are no goals. The attached screenshots show today's app: use them to know what exists, not how it should look.
Style "Obsidian Mono": background #0B0C0E, borderless surfaces #14161B, text #F3F4F6, secondary #7E8594, the habitual drawn in graphite, amber #E5A93C only when well above the habitual; no red, no neon green, no electric blue; colour never alone. Inter with tabular figures, main metric 40sp Light, section labels 11sp uppercase, 24dp between sections, no card borders. Mood: Oura. Real app icons, never letter circles. Bottom navigation with three tabs: Hoje · Análise · Ajustes.

## Prompt 2 — Hoje
Screen "Hoje" (landing tab): today's total phone time large, and how it compares to the habitual so far (calm / near / above, said in words too). Below, the most-used apps today with real icon and time; never AppTime itself, never 0-minute apps; tapping opens the app screen. No unlock or session counts here. A subtle banner at the top when tracking stopped: "O acompanhamento foi interrompido. O total de hoje pode estar incompleto." with a "Reativar" button. Also make the variant "dia 3 de 7 — seu habitual está se formando" with no comparison yet.

## Prompt 3 — Análise
Screen "Análise" with a 7 dias · 30 dias switch. Cards in order: "Uso diário" (bars per day with the habitual as a line; on 30 days, total and average of the period), "Sessões e desbloqueios" (unlock count and session lengths), "Apps mais abertos" (ranking by opens, no threshold bands), "Uso noturno" (use between 22h and 6h, no sleep claims). No research citations, no scientific text.

## Prompt 4 — Ajustes
Screen "Ajustes", in this order: live overlay preview at the top with a "Testar mensagem" button; "Visor": style gallery (Pílula, Bloco, Side rail), switch "Mostrar número", switch "Comparar com o habitual", size, intensity (nenhuma · cor/peso · contraste · cresce devagar); "Apps": full list of installed apps with search, one "monitorar" switch per app, tap a row to open the app screen; "Mensagens" on/off; "Idioma" as one row; "Privacidade e dados": data stays on the device, privacy policy, legal notice, "Apagar todos os dados"; "Permissões" only when one is missing.

## Prompt 5 — Tela do app
Screen for one app (e.g. Instagram), opened by tapping it anywhere: its time today and over recent days vs its own habitual; overlay controls for this app: "Monitorar" switch, "Posição neste app" with "voltar ao padrão", light/dark hint "Automático · Sempre escuro · Sempre claro", behaviour in fullscreen and in landscape.

## Prompt 6 — Primeira configuração
Onboarding: one large sentence per screen, buttons appear after a short pause. Steps: overlay permission, usage access, autostart (MIUI), no hibernation. Each step that opens a system screen shows a small tip line: "Depois, abra o AppTime de novo." Show the first screen, one permission step and the "pronto" screen.

## Prompt 7 — estados
Make these states: "Apagar todos os dados" confirmation saying the habitual restarts from zero; app list search with no results; "Hoje" with no data yet vs a real zero day vs partial data (three different, none looking like an error or a victory); missing permission inside Ajustes.
