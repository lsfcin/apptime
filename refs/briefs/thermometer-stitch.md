# AppTime — thermometer look: prompts for Google Stitch (paste one at a time, App/mobile mode)

## Prompt 1 — context (paste first, with the attached crops)
Android app "AppTime", Brazilian Portuguese UI. It reduces phone addiction through awareness, never blocking; there are no goals, the only reference is the user's own past. A small floating overlay shows today's minutes in the app in front ("12m") and optionally a progress cue (bar under the number, filling dot or outline) for the day vs that app's daily usual, amber #E5A93C when past it. The attached crops show it today, light and dark, as "pill" (in the status bar, 14sp, no background, dark halo, at most 52dp wide) and "block" (below the status bar, 96% black background, 17sp bold). Draw on top of them, same size and place.

## Prompt 2 — the thermometer
Add a new signal, the "thermometer": this app's use in the last 24 hours vs the 24 hours before. Three states: calm (same or less), worsening, much worse. We only ever want less: never an up arrow or anything inviting more. Label where text fits: "Uso nas últimas 24 horas". No red, no neon green, no electric blue; calm is white / automatic ink, not lavender; colour never alone (also shape, motion or text). It must not be confused with the progress cue.
Make four directions: (1) colour only plus a text state; (2) a one-way ▼ "use menos" or a calm "ok" mark; (3) a tall thin vertical line with a short horizontal tick riding it, log scale with a low midpoint; (4) a flat line when calm that starts to oscillate as it worsens, ECG-like when much worse. Then two directions of your own.

## Prompt 3 — every variant
For each direction: pill and block, each in the three states, light and dark, in three combinations: thermometer only · number + thermometer · number + progress + thermometer. Today the pill's white text vanishes on white backgrounds, and the dark halo around the text is rejected: propose ink that works on both, without a halo, and an amber shade with AA contrast on white too.

## Prompt 4 — the tab ("Aba")
A third overlay that does not exist yet: a tab attached to a screen edge, like a browser tab turned around. Hanging from the status bar (bottom corners rounded, top joined to the bar) or from a side (screen-side corners rounded, edge side straight). Show the two best directions on it, both edge positions, three states, light and dark.

## Prompt 5 — Hoje
The app's "Hoje" screen: today's total phone time large ("hoje até agora"), and beside it the same thermometer for the whole phone. Show the two best directions, three states, light and dark.
